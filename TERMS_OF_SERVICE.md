## 2. Description of Service

Arfhe Wallet is a **self-custodial** cryptocurrency wallet browser extension that:

- Generates and manages cryptographic key pairs for Ethereum and EVM-compatible blockchains
- Enables sending, receiving, and swapping digital assets
- Provides Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) privacy features via the Fhenix protocol
- Connects to decentralized applications (dApps) via WalletConnect

- We **cannot** recover lost keys, passwords, or assets.

### 3.2 Backup Responsibility

- You must securely back up your recovery phrase (mnemonic) when creating a wallet.
- If you lose your recovery phrase and password, your funds are **permanently inaccessible**.
- We strongly recommend storing your recovery phrase offline in a secure location.

### 3.3 Password Security

- We cannot reset or recover your password.
